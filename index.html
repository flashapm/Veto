Under the hood

How it knows what you spent.

A block only works inside the app. People can always open another payment app, so the product has to see every rupee that leaves your accounts, decide what it was, and react fairly. About forty rules handle that. These are the main ones.

Where the data comes from

Bank SMS

Android only. Arrives within seconds and usually carries the UPI reference number and the payee's UPI ID. This is what makes real-time catches possible.

Account Aggregator

RBI's consent-based framework for reading bank data. Slower, often hours, but complete. It's the main source on iPhone, which has no SMS access.

Email

Optional. Mostly for credit card alerts and monthly statements, which close any gaps at month end.

From a debit to a decision

Every debit is matched against payments made in the app using the UPI reference number. Duplicates from SMS, email and bank data collapse into one. Whatever is left was paid somewhere else, and gets classified.

Essentials without merchant codes

Merchant category codes would make essentials easy, but many Indian merchants take payments on personal UPI IDs, so the codes are often missing or wrong. Essentials are recognised three ways: the code when it exists, a merchant directory that learns from payments made in the app, and payees you tag yourself, like your landlord. A tagged payee has an expected amount, a tolerance and a day window, so a second "rent" payment in the same month doesn't slip through.

The colour

The app has two colour drains and shows whichever is greyer. Text and numbers keep full contrast in both.

monthly: 100% colour up to 100% of the month's budget, 0% at 130% (lasts until payday) daily: 100% colour up to 100% of today's limit, 0% at 200% (resets at midnight) shown: min(monthly, daily)

The roll

If a payment puts you over today's limit while the month is still fine, it's decided by chance. The odds get worse the further over you go and the faster you're spending this month. The draw happens on the server, and the result sticks for the same payee for 30 minutes, so retrying doesn't help.

d = today after payment ÷ daily limit c = month after payment ÷ expected spend by today chance of a block = 0.30 + 0.50 × min(d − 1, 1) + 0.20 × clamp((c − 0.8) ÷ 0.4, 0, 1) kept between 30% and 95%, plus 5% for each bypass this month

Getting caught, fairly

Evidence first

Every catch names the SMS or bank record it came from, and lists what it will cost before anything happens.

Ask when unsure

Payments to people and wallet top-ups get a question, never a roast. No answer in 24 hours counts as a yes.

Disputes win

Disputes are accepted on the spot. If one can be verified, the streak, extra questions and worse odds are all reversed.

Where the jokes stop

Fraud

"I didn't make this" switches off the personality. The screen points to your bank, the 1930 cybercrime helpline and cybercrime.gov.in, and can block UPI instantly.

Hard months

Payments to loan apps or a balance near zero before payday switch the app to a supportive tone. The monthly recap drops the roasts and the share button.

Privacy

The lock screen only ever says "I saw that." Share cards show percentages and counts, never amounts, merchants or names.
